# 🎮 Yet Another Spreadsheet That's Totally Not Excel™

## What is this thing? 🤔
Welcome to the most exciting spreadsheet application since... well, since the last spreadsheet application! This Java-based masterpiece was born from the twisted minds of Intro2CS students who thought, "Hey, you know what the world needs? ANOTHER spreadsheet program!" And here we are.

## ✨ Features That Will Blow Your Mind* 
(* Results may vary. Mind-blowing not guaranteed)

- Three exciting cell types:
  - TEXT: For when numbers are too mainstream
  - NUMBERS: Like text, but more mathematical
  - FORMULAS: Because `=A1+B1` is basically magic ✨

- A GUI that screams "I was made in Java":
  - Click cells! Watch them do things!
  - Colors that definitely won't hurt your eyes
  - Buttons that actually work (most of the time)

- Save & Load functionality:
  - Because nobody likes losing their work
  - Perfect for saving spreadsheets you'll never open again
  - Time travel not included

## 🎯 Error Handling For When Things Go Wrong
And trust me, they will go wrong:
- "ERR_FORM": When your formula looks like a cat walked on the keyboard
- "ERR_CYCLE": For when you accidentally create a spreadsheet paradox
- Other errors that will make you question your life choices

## 🚀 How to Use This Beauty

1. Run `Ex2GUI` (pray it works)
2. Click on cells until something happens
3. Type stuff:
   - Regular text: Just type like a normal person
   - Numbers: The things you used in math class
   - Formulas: Start with '=' and hope for the best
4. Watch in amazement as it actually calculates things
